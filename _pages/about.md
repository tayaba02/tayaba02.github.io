I'm a Machine Learning Scientist working across computer vision, spatial-temporal sensor analytics, and time-series forecasting. I hold an MSc in Professional Computer Science from Simon Fraser University, and I'm currently a Machine Learning Associate at the [Alberta Machine Intelligence Institute (Amii)](https://www.amii.ca/), where I advise early-stage Canadian AI startups on architecture and applied research through the Level Up program.

Most of my work has involved building models that hold up against noisy, real-world data in regulated environments, clinical imaging at UAE University, patient volume forecasting and monitoring pipelines at G42 Healthcare, credit risk data pipelines at Citi, and predictive maintenance for industrial motors at PETRONAS. I design and fine-tune networks from first principles in PyTorch and TensorFlow: CNNs, GANs, and encoder–decoder Transformers. My research is published in IEEE and Springer venues with 341+ citations.

I also teach. Over the past few years I've delivered AI and machine learning instruction to more than 1,000 learners, as a course instructor for Correlation One's Amazon Data Analytics Program, as a teaching assistant at Simon Fraser University, and as a visiting lecturer in artificial intelligence. Translating dense technical material into something people can actually build with is a large part of what I enjoy about this field.

## What I'm working on

- **Applied ML advisement** — supporting startups on LLMs, reinforcement learning, and computer vision problems
- **Spatial-temporal modelling** — anomaly detection and forecasting under distribution shift on real sensor streams
- **Generative AI systems** — modular RAG pipelines, LLM orchestration and evaluation
- **Teaching and curriculum** — building courses and guided projects that make AI concepts concrete
